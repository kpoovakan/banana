B A N A N A

Bananas are... radioactive ! And when one eats a single banana, one is exposed to more radiation than if one were to stand next to a nuclear power plant! Uh oh, I ate a banana today...

Are you joking? How are bananas radioactive??

Nope, not a joke. Bananas have lots of potassium, and the amount of potassium in a single banana is enough to significantly increase the chance of finding the rare potassium-40 in the banana.

Most potassium atoms are potassium-39, containing 19 protons and 20 neutrons. The radioisotope potassium-40 contains one extra neutron, making the neucleus unstable.

Wait, what's an isotope or radioisotope?

Elements aren't all the same; potassium-39 is different from potassium-40. This is because of isotopes. Isotopes of elements have the same number of protons and electrons, but different neutrons. Think of it like one big family of the same element, but each sibling has a different number of neutrons!

A radioisotope is an unstable isotope. Because the radioisotope is unstable, it tries to become more stable by releasing small amounts of energy. This is called radioactive decay.

Uh, how does that make bananas radioactive?

When the radioisotope potassium-40 attempts radioactive decay, the amount of energy that is released is radiation.

If I ate a banana today, does that make me radioactive??

Yupperpoodles! Actually, humans generally have more potassium than a banana. This means there is likely to be more potassium-40 in a human than in a banana. Therefore, humans are technically more dangerous than a banana...

Wait... how dangerous is a banana?

The potassium-40 in a single banana generally emits 0.1 microsieverts per banana. Due to safety rules and regulations, standing next to a nuclear power plant actually emits less radiation than a banana!

A lethal dose of radiation is a dose of radiation expected to cause the death of fifty percent of the exposed individuals within thirty days. The lethal dose ranges from four to five sieverts, emitted within an extremely short period of time.

If a single banana emits 0.1 microsieverts of radiation, then 5 sieverts worth of bananas is 50000000 bananas! Do you like bananas enough to eat fifty million bananas all at once?
